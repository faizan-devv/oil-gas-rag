WELL COMPLETION REPORT SUMMARY

Well ID: WELL-3120-SE
Operator: Caldera Offshore
Completion Date: 2024-07-05
Measured Depth (m): 4493.0
Formation: Bunter
Completion Status: Successful

1. Summary

Well WELL-3120-SE, operated by Caldera Offshore in the Bunter formation, was successfully completed on 2024-07-05. The well was completed according to plan and handed over to production. Despite encountering several operational challenges, including perforating gun misfires, a failed packer setting, and a stuck logging tool, the completion was ultimately successful. The issues required additional time and effort but did not prevent the well from achieving its intended outcome.

2. Operations Overview

The completion of WELL-3120-SE followed a standard sequence, beginning with casing operations and progressing through perforation, packer setting, and wireline logging. The well was drilled to a measured depth of 4493.0 m and completed in the Bunter formation. A series of perforating runs were conducted to establish the desired flow paths. A production packer was set to isolate the production zone, and open-hole logging was performed to assess formation characteristics. The well was then handed over to production after all completion activities were completed.

3. Issues Encountered

A perforating gun misfire occurred during run 4, with the guns across the interval 1318-1329 m MD failing to fire. This required an additional perforating run to ensure the desired interval was properly stimulated. A second issue involved the production packer, which failed to set on the first attempt at 4205 m MD. The packer was retrieved, redressed, and set successfully on the second run, which added 15 hours of rig time. Finally, a wireline logging tool became stuck at 3161 m MD, necessitating a fishing operation that lasted 19 hours to recover the tool. Each of these issues required additional planning and operational adjustments to ensure the completion was completed successfully.

4. Recommendations

To prevent similar issues in future operations, it is recommended to verify detonator continuity at the surface and carry a backup firing head for each perforating run. Packer setting tools should be function-tested at the surface before being run in hole. Additionally, a wiper trip should be conducted and the hole conditioned prior to open-hole wireline logging to reduce the risk of tool sticking.
